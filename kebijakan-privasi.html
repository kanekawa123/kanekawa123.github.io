Privacy Policy & Pinterest API Terms

Effective Date: November 9, 2026 | Organization: Kanekawa Official (https://kanekawa123.github.io)

1. Information Collection & Use: Kanekawa operates a public fashion curation portal. We do not collect, store, or sell personal data, login credentials, or payment details from visitors browsing our catalog.

2. Pinterest API Integration & OAuth 2.0 Data Usage: Our internal publishing application (Kanekawa Catalog Publisher) connects to the official Pinterest API v5 strictly for first-party account management. Specifically, our application uses OAuth 2.0 authorization solely to authenticate our own official Pinterest Business account, read our own boards and Pins (boards:read, pins:read), and publish or schedule curated fashion Pins to our own boards (boards:write, pins:write). We do not request, access, store, or share personal data, boards, or Pins belonging to any third-party Pinterest users.

3. Data Storage, Security & Retention: OAuth 2.0 access and refresh tokens for our own Pinterest Business account are stored locally in encrypted environment variables and are never exposed publicly or shared with third parties. Tokens and cached board metadata can be revoked or deleted at any time via Pinterest Account Security Settings.

4. Affiliate Disclosure & Third-Party Links: Kanekawa participates in the Shopee Affiliate Program. Product links on our website and Pinterest Pins direct users to verified merchants on Shopee. When you make a purchase through these links, we may earn a referral commission at no additional cost to you.

5. Contact Us: For privacy inquiries, data deletion requests, or API compliance questions, contact us at Kanekawajp@gmail.com.
